## 🎯 How It Works

### **1. Start Byte Smart**
Click **🎤 Byte Smart** button

**Byte says:**
- "Hello! What can I do for you?"
- "Hi! Byte here, ready to help!"
- "Hey! What do you need?"
- "Namaste! How can I assist?"
- "Yes! What's the task?"

*(Random greeting each time)*

### **2. Say Wake Word**
Say: **"byte"**

**Byte responds:**
- "Hello! What can I do for you?"
- "Got it!"
- "On it!"

*(Acknowledges and waits for command)*

### **3. Give Command**
Say: **"open chatgpt"**

**Byte responds:**
- "Got it!" or "Sure thing!" or "On it!"
- Executes the command
- Shows progress in output logs

### **4. Byte Asks for Feedback**
**Byte says:**
- "Anything else?"
- "What's next?"
- "Need anything else, or should I sleep?"
- "Can I help with something else?"
- "More work, or should I rest?"

### **5. Continue or Sleep**

**Option A - Continue:**
- Say: **"byte"** (new command)
- Say: **"open github"** (direct command)
- Byte continues working

**Option B - Sleep:**
- Say: **"no thanks"** or **"sleep"** or **"nothing"**
- Byte says: "Going to sleep. Say 'byte' to wake me!"
- Button changes back to 🎤 Byte Smart
